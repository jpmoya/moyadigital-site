## Strategy

**Current positioning:** "Meet Kai. Your AI wellness coach." — broader than before but still generic. The site already has strong gut content (Core Problem 1, Digestion feature) but it's buried among other features.

**Proposed positioning:** Lead with digestive challenges + training. Broaden the hero to anyone serious about fitness who deals with gut issues. Then go deep on IBS in a dedicated section — the way Injuries already gets its own Core Problem section.

**Why broader than "IBS":** Many people with gut issues don't identify as having IBS. They say "my stomach is messed up," "my digestion is trash," "everything I eat wrecks me." Leading with "digestive challenges" captures this larger group. The dedicated IBS section catches the ones who *do* identify.

**Who this page speaks to first:** Someone who trains seriously and can't figure out why their gut keeps derailing their progress.

**Who it still speaks to:** The other personas (Gains Chaser, Data-Driven Lifter, Injured Athlete, Tracking Newcomer) appear throughout and benefit from the cross-domain positioning.

**Primary action:** Sign up and start logging (free).

**Voice:** Direct, confident, grounded. Empathetic on gut issues — never dismissive. Short sentences. Specific over vague. Uses "you" and "your."

**Positioning principle:** Kai helps people find *their own* patterns. Everything is case-by-case. Kai doesn't prescribe universal fixes. It surfaces what's happening in *your* data so *you* can make better decisions.

## Reddit Research: Key Language Patterns

Direct quotes and recurring phrases from the research, organized by theme.

### Pain Points (their words)

- "Trying to bulk is a nightmare when everything makes my stomach hurt"
- "My routine is toilet, gym, protein shake then toilet"
- "I can pretty much do any workout. The problem is meal and meal timing"
- "I was the fittest I've ever been, looking like a Greek god. My physique meant everything to me" → then lost it all to a flare
- "Every exercise that will actually help me lose weight triggers my stomach"
- "I've tried getting into fitness to lose weight and IBS seems to ruin that"
- "Supersetting every exercise with a shit break"
- "My biggest barrier to progress is my poor digestion"
- "I can't eat enough to actually make progress"
- "Health is a crown only the sick see"

### What They've Tried (and it didn't work)

- Elimination diets (low-FODMAP, gluten-free, dairy-free) — "still don't know what's wrong"
- Food diaries — tracking meals without connecting to training/sleep
- Cutting foods for months without improvement
- Multiple apps (Monash, Cara, mySymptoms) — track food + symptoms only
- Seeing gastros who say "it's probably IBS, try low-FODMAP"

### What They Should Start Considering

- Gut microbiome dysbiosis, parasites, fungal overgrowth
- Imbalanced meals — too much fat and/or not enough fiber and/or not enough bulk
- Too many push-hard-then-crash-and-burn cycles
- Impact of both psychological and physiological stress on digestion — both take blood away from digestion
- Not eating slowly and not being in a parasympathetic state
- Eating and going to sleep right away
- Loading up on coffee right before/after eating
- Too much coffee or alcohol creating inflammation and swelling

### Eureka Moments

- "Food wasn't the issue — my digestive system was dysregulated"
- "It took me 13 years and an LLM to get a diagnosis"
- Meal timing matters more than meal content for many: "I can't go after eating... I usually exercise fasted"
- Protein powder switches (whey concentrate → isolate → plant-based) as individual breakthroughs
- Moderate, consistent exercise helping some people — but highly individual, not a universal fix

**Key insight:** Everything is case-by-case. People need to track, experiment, and find *their own* patterns. This is exactly what Kai enables.

### Emotional Texture

- Guilt about canceling plans
- Fear of flare-ups preventing gym attendance
- Body image loss ("disgusted when I look in the mirror")
- Defeatist attitude ("talking about it doesn't change anything")
- Isolation ("stopped going out")
- Financial burden ($3,000+ in testing, all inconclusive)

## Copy Principles Used

| Principle | How it's applied |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Community language | "nightmare," "thought about quitting," "still don't know what's wrong" — from Reddit |
| Broad hero, specific sections | Hero uses "gut" / "digestive challenges." Dedicated IBS section catches self-identified IBS sufferers. |
| Case-by-case positioning | Kai helps you find YOUR patterns, not universal fixes. |
| Name the gap, not the enemy | Food diaries are incomplete, not bad. Doctors aren't wrong. |
| Show the cross-domain insight | Every section demonstrates Kai connecting training + nutrition + sleep + gut |
| Empathy without pity | Uses their own words. Never says "we understand how hard it is" |
| Delayed trigger education | Reinforces that triggers are often delayed by days |
