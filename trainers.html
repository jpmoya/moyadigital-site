FAQ

Common questions.

Does Kai replace the nutritional guidance I give my clients?

No. Kai doesn't write meal plans or override your programming. It helps your client follow the plan you already gave them by identifying which foods trigger their gut and suggesting swaps that still hit the macros you set. You stay in control of the nutrition strategy. Kai handles the gut side you don't have visibility into.

What if my client has a diagnosed condition like IBD or celiac?

Kai is designed for IBS and functional gut issues, not for managing diagnosed inflammatory bowel disease, celiac, or other clinical conditions. If your client has a diagnosed GI condition, they should be working with a gastroenterologist. Kai is a wellness tool that helps people understand patterns between their food, symptoms, and lifestyle. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or replace medical care.
