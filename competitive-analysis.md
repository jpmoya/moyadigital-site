## Bevel Health (bevel.health)

**What they do well:**

1. **Integrations as a visual section** — "Works with" section showing Apple Watch, Oura, Garmin, Amazfit logos right after the hero. Apple Watch Spotlight and New & Noteworthy badges. Makes ecosystem support a selling point, not a footnote. → **Implementing now.**

2. **Features are scannable, not walls of text** — Each feature gets one sentence + a phone mockup showing real UI. The "And that's not all" grid lists 9 more features as icon + one-liner. No paragraphs.

3. **Social proof is front and center** — "4.8 / 28.6k ratings globally" in the nav. "Join over 1 million members" with real photos. 5 app store testimonials with usernames and dates. → **Adding placeholder for first beta tester quote.**

4. **Device ecosystem is explicit** — Not just mentioned — shown with logos and badges.

5. **AI is positioned as a layer, not the product** — "Go deeper with Bevel Intelligence" comes AFTER core tracking features. Cites real sources (PubMed, ScienceDirect). Builds trust.

**What they DON'T have that Xaki does:**

- No gut/digestion focus — generic recovery/strain/sleep
- No cross-domain pattern detection (their AI answers questions but doesn't proactively surface multi-factor connections)
- No injury awareness
- No founder story — polished but impersonal
- No IBS or digestive health positioning — wide-open lane for Xaki
