## 3. VISUAL — Pattern Recognition Cards

### Card 1 — The Gut Sufferer

**Domains:** Exercise · Meals · Bathroom trips

**The pattern Kai spotted:**
> Over the last two months, your gut acts up after stretches of heavy exercise — and it's worst when those days come with fat-rich, low-fiber meals and short nights of sleep. It's not one thing. It's the pile-up.

**What Kai suggests:**
> On heavy exercise days, add fiber to your meals (veggies or a spoon of flaxseed), increase healthy carbs (rice, potatoes) and go lighter on fat-heavy foods (cheese, sausages, even nuts). Aim for 7+ hours of sleep, drink electrolytes, and slot a rest or mobility day between hard sessions.

---

### Card 2 — The Gains Chaser

**Domains:** Meals · Exercise · Sleep

**The pattern Kai spotted:**
> On your exercise days, you're eating only about 2/3 the protein your body needs to recover. On those same weeks, your deep sleep dropped noticeably and you struggled with constipation — your body was under stress and couldn't recharge.

**What Kai suggests:**
> Pick a protein-rich snack and add it to your meal plan every single day, e.g. Greek yogurt with granola or a protein shake. Have it in the morning or afternoon, not late at night, so it fuels recovery without disrupting sleep.

## 8. FAQ

**Is Kai a doctor?**
> No. Kai is an AI wellness coach, not a medical professional. For anything clinical, see an actual doctor. Kai helps you make better decisions about nutrition, sleep, and exercise — not diagnose or treat conditions.

**Is my health data safe?**
> Your data stays on your profile. Not shared. Not sold. Not used to train models.

**Do I have to log everything perfectly?**
> No. Start with one thing — meals, exercise, or bathroom trips — and add more over time. Kai gets sharper the more you log, but it doesn't need perfection to be useful.

**What if I already use other apps?**
> Kai doesn't replace them — it connects the dots between them. Import data from Apple Health, Garmin, and more integrations are coming soon.

**Does it cost anything?**
> Core tracking, personalized recommendations and even conversations with Kai are all free. Longer conversations with Kai that knows your complete history will eventually be available via subscription.

**How is this different from Cara / mySymptoms / food diary apps?**
> Those apps primarily track meals and symptoms. Kai tracks your exercise, nutrition, bathroom trips, sleep, and stress — at the same time — and finds the patterns across all of them. Your gut doesn't flare from one thing. Kai sees the full picture.
